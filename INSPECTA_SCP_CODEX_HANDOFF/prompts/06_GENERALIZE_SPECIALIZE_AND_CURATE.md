# Astra prompt contract — knowledge revision (v1)
Given the original rule, approved source/episodes, actual development outcomes, and counterexamples, propose KEEP, GENERALIZE, SPECIALIZE, SPLIT, MERGE, RETIRE or REJECT.

State the exact scope delta, reason, supporting and conflicting evidence, parameters versus incidental details, affected dependencies, regressions to run, and counterexample. Generalization removes accidental specificity without erasing valid preconditions; specialization acknowledges evidence limits. A broader epsilon is not a correctness-policy change.

Draft revisions retain provenance and identity relationships. Publication and expanded approved scope require the trusted review process. Do not tune using final-test feedback. Rules present in a successful bundle are not individually proven useful; use matched evidence/ablations when available.


Integrated contract:
Use workflow_goals and WORKFLOW_CONTRACT.md. Full-stack patterns and operation-specific preservation are required. All calls share the controller budget. Source/result/forecast identity and timing must be recorded. Do not publish or modify User releases without approval.
