                        independence claim

                        model calls

    approval            Local human review; never implicit model approval
    budget              Request/apply an exact approved budget amendment

    plot                Export actual run metrics as CSV/JSON and PNG/SVG/PDF

                        required

                        original source

    requirements        Source-backed obligation ledger and independent review

                        controller
